WATCHMAKING

Why Mechanical Watches Still Matter

By TIMELESS EDITORIAL

Mechanical watches represent centuries of engineering, craftsmanship and human creativity.

Unlike a simple digital device, a mechanical watch contains hundreds of tiny components working together. This combination of engineering and craftsmanship is what makes traditional watchmaking so fascinating.

GUIDE

Understanding Automatic Watches

By TIMELESS EDITORIAL

Automatic watches use the movement of your wrist to keep the mechanism running.

Inside an automatic watch is a small rotor. As the wearer moves their wrist, the rotor moves and helps wind the mainspring of the watch.

HISTORY

The Evolution of the Wristwatch

By TIMELESS EDITORIAL

From practical military instruments to luxury statements, wristwatches have changed dramatically.

Wristwatches became increasingly popular during the twentieth century and eventually became symbols of personal style, engineering and status.
